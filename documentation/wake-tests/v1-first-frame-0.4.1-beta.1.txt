slot.advance 0.4.1-beta.1: first-frame resume timing (V1), 15 October 2026 (UTC)

Recorded with the read-only resume-timing watcher (p0r-watch.sh, from the "0.5.0 resume timing"
kit, unchanged since the 0.4.0 baseline) on the 0.4.1-beta.1 candidate (program SHA-256
085e5180...7b26; slot.log: "slot advance 0.4.1-beta.1 (497c8e5)"). Real lid closes of about
15 seconds with a game running, on battery.

  Session A (boot at 06:12 UTC): Wi-Fi off (the default). 11 wakes, all timed.
  Session B (boot at 06:21 UTC): wifi=on, the driver kept loaded. 10 wakes; the watcher timed
  7 of them (it misses a wake when the lid opens too soon after it settles).

How to read it: every time is on the kernel's own log clock, in milliseconds from "resume
start" (the kernel line "Enabling non-boot CPUs", the first thing the kernel logs on waking)
to the first GPU interrupt, which is slot.advance's first rendered frame. The time between the
lid opening and the kernel starting to resume is not logged, so it is not included. Each wake's
summary line begins "TIMELINE (ms from resume start)". first-frame.py recalculates the medians.

The 0.4.0 baseline is R2, the same kit and procedure on 0.4.0 on 14 October 2026: Wi-Fi off,
10 wakes, first frame 1236 to 1270 ms (median 1251); wifi=on, 9 wakes, median 820 ms. R2's own
report was not kept in the repository; its figures are recorded in first-frame.py.

Below this header, everything is unedited device output: the watcher's report for both
sessions, then slot.log for session A and session B.
